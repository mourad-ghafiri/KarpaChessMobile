KarpaChess is a chess-learning app for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by
**Mourad Ghafiri** ("we"). This policy covers the app and this website,
karpachess.com. It says what happens to your information when you use them.

**In short: the KarpaChess app collects no personal data, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else. This website sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics, ads or trackers. The only personal data involved are the technical logs our web host keeps when you visit, and anything you choose to send us.**

## Who is responsible

Mourad Ghafiri, a private individual, publishes the app and this website and
is responsible (the "data controller") for the little personal data described
below. You can reach him at
[contact@karpachess.com](mailto:contact@karpachess.com).

## The KarpaChess app

### What we collect

Nothing. KarpaChess has:

- no account and no sign-in;
- no advertising;
- no analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting;
- no tracking, and no third-party code that does any of these.

The app never connects to the internet. There is no server for it to send
anything to.

### What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app stores these in its own private storage on your device:

- **Your progress:** lessons, the puzzle rating and results, points, level and
  streak.
- **Your settings:** language, theme, board, pieces, sounds, difficulty, time
  control, and the player name if you set one.
- **Games:**
  - a practice game you have not finished;
  - the game open in the Studio, with your drawings on it;
  - games you import into the Studio library.
- **Pictures you choose:** a copy of the player picture you set, and of any
  pictures you give to the players of a Studio game.

None of this leaves your device through the app. It is not shared with anyone,
and we cannot see it.

Your device's own backups may include it: iCloud or Finder backups on iPhone
and iPad, and Android's backup service. Apple or Google handle those backups
under your account, and we have no access to them. You control them in your
device's settings.

### Pictures and files

When you choose a picture for your player profile or for a Studio player, the
app opens your device's photo picker. It copies only the picture you pick into
its own storage. It does not read the rest of your photo library, and it never
uses the camera.

When you import a game into the Studio, the app reads only the file you choose
or the text you paste.

### Deleting your data

- **Reset progress & settings**, at the bottom of Settings, erases your
  progress, your settings and your player picture.
- An imported game can be removed from the Studio library.
- Closing a game in the Studio clears the saved Studio session.
- **Deleting the app** removes everything it stored.

The app never sends us your data, so there is nothing from it for us to
access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You can still write to us
with any question (see **Contact** below).

## This website

### No cookies, no tracking

Our pages set no cookies and store nothing on your device. They have no
analytics, no advertising, no social-media buttons and no embedded content
from other websites. Fonts, images and scripts are all served from
karpachess.com itself, so opening a page sends nothing to Google or to any
other third party. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to
consent to.

The one exception comes from our host's security systems, not from our pages.
To tell people from automated traffic, Cloudflare adds a small script to the
pages it serves, and it may store two cookies in your browser: `cf_clearance`,
which records the result of that check, and `__cf_bm`, which expires after 30
minutes without activity. Cloudflare describes them as strictly necessary to
protect the site. They are used only for that, never for advertising, and
never to follow you from one website to another. The CNIL, France's data
protection authority, treats cookies used only to secure a website as
needing no consent, which is why we tell you about them here instead of
asking.

### Server logs kept by our host

Whenever your browser asks for a page, the server automatically receives some
technical information. Our host, **Cloudflare, Inc.** (United States),
receives your IP address, the date and time, the page asked for, your
browser's user agent and the page you came from. Cloudflare processes this on
our behalf, as our processor, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse
and attacks. We do not use it to identify, follow or profile you, and we do
not combine it with any other data.

- **Purpose:** delivering the website and keeping it secure.
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** only as long as Cloudflare needs it for these purposes, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Links to other websites

The download buttons link to Apple's App Store and to Google Play, and other
links lead to sites such as GitHub. Nothing is loaded from another site until
you follow a link to it. Once you are there, that site's own privacy policy
applies.

## When you contact us

If you write to [contact@karpachess.com](mailto:contact@karpachess.com), we
receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message.
We use them only to reply to you, based on our legitimate interest in
answering your message (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). The message passes through
Cloudflare's email routing and is stored by our mailbox provider on our
behalf. We keep a conversation only as long as we need it to handle your
request, and delete it no later than 12 months after our last exchange,
unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Please do not send us sensitive
information by email.

If you report a problem on GitHub instead, your report is public and is
handled under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Sharing and transfers

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We do not use it for advertising
or profiling, and no automated decisions are made about you. The only
recipients are the service providers described above, for hosting and email,
which act on our instructions.

Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States) is
based in the United States and runs servers around the world, so server logs
may be processed outside your country. For data leaving the European Economic
Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Cloudflare relies on its
certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss
extensions, with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses as a
fallback.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and
ask us to correct or delete it. Email
[contact@karpachess.com](mailto:contact@karpachess.com) and we will reply
within one month. We hold almost nothing: the app sends us no data, and server
logs do not identify you by name. So tell us what you are looking for, and we
will help as far as we can.

### Europe and the United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United
Kingdom, you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or
erased, to restrict its use, to object to its use (including use based on our
legitimate interest), and to receive it in a portable format. You can also
complain to your data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL
([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/)). French residents can also tell us what
should happen to their data after their death.

### United States

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for
targeted advertising. Because this site does no tracking at all, it behaves
the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy
Control signal: there is nothing to switch off. No third party collects
information about your activity across websites through this site, or through
the app.

### China

If you are in mainland China, the Personal Information Protection Law gives
you the right to know about and decide on the processing of your personal
information, to restrict or refuse it, to access, copy, correct or delete it,
and to ask us to explain these rules. The processing outside China is the one
described above: Cloudflare (whose name, address, role and purpose are given
in **Sharing and transfers**) handles server logs and email routing for us. To
use your rights, for data held by us or by Cloudflare, write to the address
below.

## Children

KarpaChess is suitable for all ages. The app collects no data from anyone,
children included. The website is designed for a general audience and asks no
one for any information. Its server logs are used only to deliver and secure
the site, never to contact, profile or advertise to anyone, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child
has written to us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete the message, and
we will.
