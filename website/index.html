How it teaches

Every lesson ends with proof.

Each of the hundred lessons follows the same three beats, so an idea goes from something you have seen to something you can do.

  1. See it

    The idea, on the board

    Arrows and moves played out for you, with a short explanation. Each beat teaches one thing.

  2. Play it

    Now you make the moves

    You play the idea yourself. Stuck? The hint is a sentence the lesson's author wrote for exactly that position.

  3. Own it

    Prove it somewhere new

    A puzzle on a position you have not seen shows the pattern is yours. It goes into your Pattern Book, and Sharpen brings it back before you forget it.

Features

Six ways to get better.

Learn

A hundred lessons, from setting up the board to the Greek gift and the Lucena bridge. Each one counts toward your level, your rank and your daily streak.

Puzzles

A rated trainer: 240 puzzles in twenty packs, from 600 to 2000. Your first try moves your rating. After that, replay any puzzle to beat your best.

Play

Games against Stockfish 19 at four strengths, timed with a real clock or untimed. A game you leave is still there when you come back.

Review

Every move of a finished game graded, with the turning point explained:

  • Best
  • Good
  • Inaccuracy
  • Mistake
  • Blunder

Studio

240 short master games in 24 collections, plus your own PGN files, with their comments and variations kept. Brilliant moves are flagged, and you can draw on the board.

A hint when you ask

One hint button, answered on your device. It looks at material, king safety, pawn structure, development and loose pieces, and says what matters.

Privacy

Nothing you do leaves your device.

KarpaChess collects no personal data. There is no server for it to send anything to.

No account

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

No internet

The app never connects. Stockfish thinks on your device.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Yours alone

Progress, settings and games stay on your device. Deleting the app removes them all.
